Less waste in every box we make.

Environmental sustainability is a core focus for us. Our management keeps reviewing and improving production to cut waste, save energy and keep our approach eco-friendly.

We follow market trends and learn from industry leaders to keep costs sensible without lowering quality, and our experienced team works to proven systems to get the most from every sheet of paper.

Reduce, reuse, recycle.

  • Reduce

    We use renewable energy to depend less on thermal power, and design box templates with efficient cutting patterns that leave fewer paper scraps. Our goal is to cut paper waste by 75%.

  • Reuse

    We save energy and materials to keep a sustainable cycle going. Waste from manufacturing goes back to paper mills for reuse, and we ask customers to return used shippers.

  • Recycle

    We recycle paper and replace lamination with water-based aqueous and UV coatings. Our packaging uses materials with high recycled content.

Our commitment to nature.

We started with a sense of responsibility for sustainability and environmental protection, and our business is built around protecting the planet while offering quality packaging. We make wide use of recyclable and biodegradable materials to cut waste and shrink our carbon footprint.

Energy-efficient processes help us contribute to a circular economy, where packaging materials are reused and repurposed. For us, sustainability is a responsibility, so we keep developing greener packaging that keeps product quality high.

  • Recycled and recyclable materials, so every box has minimal environmental impact
  • Energy-efficient manufacturing that minimises energy consumption
  • Responsible production and waste reduction at every stage
