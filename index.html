Made from plate to pallet in our own plants.

  1. Artwork is prepared and printing plates are made in our own pre-press, so print matches your approved design.

  2. Mitsubishi presses print four colours on paper and board from 50 to 450 GSM, using flexography and offset.

  3. Corrugation machines and fully automatic flute laminating machines build the board. Lamination adds a glossy finish and protects against moisture, dirt and wear.

  4. Die-cutters cut each format to shape with precision, from simple trays to complex cartons.

  5. Coaters, folder gluers and stitching machines complete the box, ready for packing and dispatch.

  6. Our in-house lab tests every product before it leaves. Each dispatch ships with a test report attached to the invoice.

Our sustainability work

It started with one shoe box.

In 1997, Mr. V J Cherian started Vettiyil with a 500 sq ft workshop and a single product: shoe boxes for local footwear brands. His son, Mr. George Cherian, joined him to widen the range for healthcare, food, electronics and other industries.

Today the company runs three plants and more than 80 machines. It still treats every order the way it treated that first box.
