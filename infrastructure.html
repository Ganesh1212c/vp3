To international standards, from plate to finished carton.

Our printing and lamination meet international standards. Flexography and offset printing give crisp, vibrant, multi-colour designs that reflect the quality of our customers' brands.

Lamination adds durability and a glossy look, and an extra layer of protection against moisture, dirt and wear. The box keeps its appearance through transit.

Regular quality checks run from material sourcing to the final finish. We aim for packaging that combines durability, good looks and a lighter footprint.

Innovation at every step.

Our in-house laboratory tests all our products before they leave the facility. Testing in-house also helps us cut wastage during production.

Management keeps reviewing the process to minimise wastage, conserve energy and stay eco-friendly, and our experienced team works to proven systems to get the most from every shift.
