Corrugated boxes.

Corrugated packaging is built from layers: an inner layer, an outer layer and a fluted layer between them. Together they give the product strength and cushioning.

  • For shipping, storage and e-commerce
  • Made with recycled paper
  • Strength-tested in our in-house lab
Build a corrugated box

Printed corrugated boxes.

Flexography and offset printing on our Mitsubishi presses give sharp, vibrant multi-colour artwork, on paper and board from 50 to 450 GSM.

  • Four-colour printing on a wide range of board
  • Lamination for gloss and protection against moisture and wear
  • Water-based aqueous and UV coatings as a lower-waste finish

Grey board boxes and mono cartons.

Mono cartons are used for compact packaging and are highly customisable. They suit a large number of applications across industries. Grey board boxes give products a firmer, more premium finish.

  • Compact packaging for a wide range of products
  • Made to your size and artwork
